# Changelog

## v1.0 — 2026-11-16

First public release.

- Quotes and invoices on phone or computer, saved privately in your own Claude account
- Guided setup: attach two files to a Claude chat and type "Build my Quick Quote"
- Products & services with per-item, hourly, time-per-piece, flat and percentage pricing
- Quote → invoice in one tap with linked numbers; optional separate sequences, date-based numbers, or the year in front (Q-26-001)
- Sales tax: added, included or not taxable; multiple tax locations with itemized rates; optional tax on shipping
- Per-quote deposits, optional payment tracking, clickable pay link and QR code on PDFs
- PDF quotes and invoices with your logo and colors
- CSV exports: sales summary (week/month), sales tax summary, invoices, number register
- Backup, restore and cleanup of old records
- PDF and QR libraries loaded from jsDelivr with integrity hashes
- Clear notice when data can only be saved on the current device (for example, on Claude's free plan)
